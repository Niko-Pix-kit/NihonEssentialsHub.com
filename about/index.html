About us

A company built around useful ideas.

Nihon Essentials Hub G.K. is an independent company based in Fukuoka, Japan, active since 2023.

We work across physical products, software and independent publishing. Our role connects design and development with the practical work of bringing a product or a creative work to market.

How we work

01

Start with a real need

We focus on clear purposes and practical uses.

02

Connect creation with distribution

We consider how a product or a work will be presented, delivered and discovered from the outset.

03

Respect independent ownership

Authors and developers retain the rights to their work. Our role in distribution and marketing is defined by agreement with each creator.
