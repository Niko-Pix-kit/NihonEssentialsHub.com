Cookies & your choices

We remember your cookie choices. You can separately allow language storage and Google Analytics for audience measurement.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text values stored by your browser. We use them to remember your choices and, if you agree, to measure use of the website.

Essential cookie: cookie_consent

cookie_consent stores your separate choices for language and analytics for 180 days. It contains no unique visitor identifier.

Optional cookie: site_language

site_language remembers the language you explicitly select for up to 365 days if you enable language storage.

Google Analytics

With your consent, Google Analytics measures site usage, such as page views. Google receives technical connection and browser data. The _ga and _ga_LHR5R3H8ZP cookies distinguish browsers and maintain session state for up to 180 days. Nihon Essentials Hub configures page views without URL query strings or fragments, limits the referring URL to its origin, and disables advertising features in the site tag.

If you refuse or cookies are blocked

The site works if you refuse or block cookies. The page address and tab session can preserve your language choice. The tab can also remember your cookie decision if cookies are blocked. Automatic language selection uses your browser settings.

Hosting and external websites

Our hosting provider receives technical connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure pages. Amazon and Pix-Kit apply their own policies when you follow their links.

Change or delete your choices

Use Manage preferences in the footer to change either choice at any time. Turning off a category removes its website cookies. You can also delete or block cookies in your browser.

Website operator and contact

Nihon Essentials Hub G.K., Fukuoka, Japan, operates this website. For privacy questions, use the email link on our Contact page. This policy describes the implemented behavior; it is not a certification of universal legal compliance.
