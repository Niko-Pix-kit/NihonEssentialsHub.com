Nihon Essentials Hub

Our Team

A small company. A slightly unhinged cast. Some very real projects.

Six versions of the founder. And a dachshund to keep our feet on the ground.

Nicolas Loffredo

CEO & Founder

Nicolas studied in France, earned a PhD in chemistry and moved to Japan. A researcher, then a director in artificial intelligence and data analysis, he has worked in computer vision and medical imaging. He founded Nihon Essentials Hub to design, develop and launch his own projects. Away from work, he has an unexplained passion for octopuses and takoyaki. Research into the cause continues, usually around lunchtime.

MottoI hate meetings. Don’t schedule one to discuss it.

Niklas Stack

Full Stack Developer

Niklas would rather spend three evenings automating a thirty-second task than do it twice. From front end to servers, everything eventually connects to one of his Python scripts. His mornings resemble the opening of Back to the Future: coffee starts itself, courtesy of the programs and robots he has built. The only manual step left is reminding him to drink it.

MottoIf you do it more than once, automate it.

Nico Droid

Android App Manager

Nico builds Android apps in Kotlin and Java and tests them on real phones. Between releases, he goes back to saving the world in Secret of Mana. Retro gaming taught him that a good idea can outlive generations of consoles. His side quest is olives. No amount of data can explain how many he eats.

MottoSave. Test. Have another olive.

Newcomer

Nicholas Swift

Apple App Manager

Nicholas is preparing our first iOS apps. He polishes interfaces, aligns every detail and remains convinced that an expensive purchase makes its owner slightly superior. Discussions with Nico Droid can get lively. In The Matrix, he would have requested the red pill in titanium, with a matching case sold separately. We watch his budget; he watches our finishes.

MottoIt’s expensive. It must be good.

Nicole Pitch

Marketing & E-commerce Manager

Nicole runs the shops, campaigns and ideas, which usually arrive three at a time. She can turn a product listing into a rocket launch, then get very serious about the cost of a click. She loves dachshunds, dressing gowns and oil. Dachshunds are life. There is no official explanation for the other two. Her megaphone mainly announces that she has “one tiny idea”.

MottoDachshunds are life.

Nikola Calibre

Product Design & Sourcing Manager

Nikola handles products, suppliers and the details nobody else noticed. She refuses to wear glasses: a magnifying glass does the job perfectly, even when she is looking for her magnifying glass. She inspects bamboo like the Terminator, but from twenty centimetres away. Dimensions, joints, finishes: nothing escapes her checks. Except whatever is on the other side of the room.

MottoGlasses? I already have a magnifying glass.

Honorary member

Watson

The CEO’s right-hand dog

The CEO’s right-hand dog, despite having rather short legs. Watson writes no code, negotiates no deals and never suggests a meeting. He stays beside the desk and regularly reminds everyone that a walk cannot be downloaded. His authority comes from a very specific look, usually directed at the door or the biscuit tin.

MottoFewer meetings. More walks.
